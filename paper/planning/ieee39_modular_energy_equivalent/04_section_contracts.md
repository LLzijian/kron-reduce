# Section contracts

## Section 1: Engineering problem and gap

- Purpose: define the need for one post-clear engineering equivalent with accurate trajectories and direct stability assessment.
- Inputs: scope, canon literature facts, evidence table.
- Allowed claims: limitations of existing evidence and the registered research question.
- Forbidden claims: completed final model, general unknown-system guarantee, publication readiness.
- Required evidence: current accepted anchors and failed certificate/accuracy examples.
- Validation checklist: the gap is falsifiable; IEEE39 is clearly the main evidence; synthetic evidence is not presented as performance.

## Section 2: Single-model module union

- Purpose: describe candidate generation by excitation view, union composition, joint fitting and whole-block deletion.
- Inputs: algorithm contract and P3 accepted artifacts.
- Allowed claims: one model, no source routing, explicit block equations and registered deletion rule.
- Forbidden claims: device-level meaning without identifiability, fixed module count as success.
- Required evidence: module registry, support table, role audit and counterfactual block effects.
- Validation checklist: data flow contains no forbidden channel; every block has an equation and gate; final artifact reports one model.

## Section 3: Spatial-resolution decision

- Purpose: establish why this corpus uses ten-generator coordinates.
- Inputs: focused high-order cluster validation.
- Allowed claims: corpus-specific validated refusal to cluster.
- Forbidden claims: clustering is universally invalid or the 45-edge support is the physical transmission topology.
- Required evidence: loss components, bootstrap support, role/sensitivity stability.
- Validation checklist: negative result is reported as a valid decision; test remains locked.

## Section 4: Identification and closed-loop evaluation

- Purpose: explain weak-form fitting, causal input, source/fault-line balancing and F1 selection.
- Inputs: accepted P2 contract/replay, P3/P4 artifacts.
- Allowed claims: registered training and selection procedures and accepted F1 results.
- Forbidden claims: observed-Pm as primary causal evidence, test or multi-fold generalization before P6.
- Required evidence: P2 role/scale hashes, baseline checkpoints, per-case metrics, aggregate metrics, failure/COI audits and paired fault-line uncertainty.
- Validation checklist: all main-table methods use ten-machine coordinates and causal input; 1/5/10 s reported; angle/speed separated; tail metrics included; observed-Pm is separate; baseline comparison is matched.

## Section 5: Modal interpretation

- Purpose: distinguish shared equation poles from excitation-local observability.
- Inputs: synthetic modal gate, IEEE39 excitation-view support, five-fold pole table.
- Allowed claims: only modes passing support, frequency, damping, MAC and fold-replication gates.
- Forbidden claims: ordinary DMD as exact autonomous spectrum; unvalidated device semantics.
- Required evidence: mode matches, bootstrap support and fold frequency.
- Validation checklist: every named frequency maps to a model pole and a support artifact; unsupported poles remain internal.

## Section 6: Integration-free stability certificate

- Purpose: define storage, certified region, margin and Level 6 validation.
- Inputs: synthetic truth, new IEEE39 boundary audit, physical/numerical certificates.
- Allowed claims: only the name and scope earned by all gates.
- Forbidden claims: zero-miss count alone, empty-region certificate, extrapolation outside envelope.
- Required evidence: FSR upper bound, coverage, region fidelity, margin error and supply/Hessian/RK4 gates.
- Validation checklist: labels first read after freeze; independent group count explicit; certificate is non-vacuous.

## Section 7: Five-fold results and adversarial tests

- Purpose: present generalization, block stability, ablations and failure cases.
- Inputs: P6 and P7 evidence freeze.
- Allowed claims: F1–F5/seed-324 results with clustered uncertainty.
- Forbidden claims: unrun multi-seed robustness or hidden post-hoc tuning.
- Required evidence: selection markers, fold table, worst-case examples, claim-to-artifact map.
- Validation checklist: core blocks appear in at least four folds; no fold is hidden; figures have source data.

## Section 8: Limitations and engineering boundary

- Purpose: state envelope, data limitations, simulator provenance and next data requests.
- Inputs: unresolved canon claims and negative evidence.
- Allowed claims: exact evidence boundaries and concrete data needs.
- Forbidden claims: vague assurances or promises that replace failed gates.
- Required evidence: refusal statistics, sample independence audit, unresolved mechanism list.
- Validation checklist: every limitation maps to a gate or missing observation; language is direct.
